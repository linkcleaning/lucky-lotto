## 앱 심사 정보 · 메모 (Review Notes, 영어로 붙여넣기)

Lucky Lotto (행운로또) is a personal lottery ticket manager for Korea's Lotto 6/45. It does NOT sell lottery tickets, take payments, or offer any real-money or simulated gambling. It is not affiliated with Dong-hang Lottery (the official operator).

Core features (native):
1. Scan the QR code printed on a paper Lotto ticket (native camera barcode scanner) – the round and numbers are saved on the device.
2. Automatic result check per round: each line shows how many numbers matched and the prize rank; a summary table shows ranks 1–5.
3. Local notification after the Saturday draw for rounds the user has saved (scheduled on-device, no server).
4. Past winning numbers list (public data, downloaded as a static JSON file).
5. Optional number generator weighted by numbers meaningful to the user (birthdays, car plate), with haptics and native share sheet. The slot-machine style reel animation is purely decorative: there are no coins, bets, credits, payouts or rewards of any kind — the reels simply stop on the generated lottery numbers.
6. "Just for fun" daily luck card – clearly labeled as entertainment; the app states that every number has the same odds.

How to test without a real ticket:
- Open https://linkcleaning.github.io/lucky-lotto/store/review-qr.html on another screen.
- In the app tap "산 로또 QR 찍고 당첨 확인" (Scan ticket QR) and scan a sample code.
  · Round 1247 sample: shows the result immediately (5th and 4th prize lines).
  · Round 1248 sample: saved as "before draw"; a notification is scheduled for after the draw.
- You can also use "사진에서 불러오기" (load from photo) or "용지 번호 직접 입력" (manual entry).

Privacy: no account, no tracking, no analytics, no ads. All data is stored only on the device. Camera is used only to read the ticket QR.
Responsible play: the app shows that lottery purchase is limited to ages 19+ in Korea and shows the national problem-gambling helpline (1336).
